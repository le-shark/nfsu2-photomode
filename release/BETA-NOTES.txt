NFSU2 Photo Mode: closed beta
=============================

Thanks for testing. Install and controls are in README.txt. This build writes a detailed log
(scripts\NFSU2PhotoMode.log, [Debug] Verbose = 1 is already on). The log starts fresh each time the game starts, so
copy it right after the problem, before you launch the game again.


What to try
-----------
Play normally and take photos. Please also try these, they are the least tested:

1.  Your settings: open photo mode in a race, then quit the game. Start it again and check that Depth of Field and
    Motion Blur in the game's Display options are still as you had them.
2.  Alt-tab out of the game while a photo is being taken, and while the view is still sharpening. Then keep using
    photo mode.
3.  A 2x photo (Resolution row), especially if your screen is 1440p or 4K. Also one with a slow shutter (motion blur).
4.  A gamepad: open with L3 + R3, move through the menu with the D-pad, take a photo with X, hide the menu with Y,
    close with B.
5.  Free roam (not only races).
6.  Open and close photo mode many times over several races. Afterwards, check that driving, the HUD, the pause
    menu (Esc) and the rear-view mirror (bumper view) are normal.
7.  Other mods you use: tell us which. Of special interest: NFSU-XtendedInput, Extra Options, CameraToolkit,
    playing without Widescreen Fix.


Known issue
-----------
With NFSU2 CameraToolkit installed, the photo mode menu may appear in the saved photos.


What to send
------------
- scripts\NFSU2PhotoMode.log
- what you did and what you expected, and a normal screenshot if something looked wrong
- your screen resolution, graphics card, and the other mods in your scripts folder
